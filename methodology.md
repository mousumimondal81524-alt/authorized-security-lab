# Security Testing Methodology

## 1. Authorization
Confirm that the target appears in the approved asset inventory and that the testing window is active.

## 2. Reconnaissance
Perform only the explicitly permitted, limited reconnaissance activities.

## 3. Validation
Validate observations without destructive actions or denial-of-service activity.

## 4. Evidence
Collect the minimum evidence necessary. Redact secrets and personal data.

## 5. Analysis
Record the affected asset, severity rationale, reproduction steps, impact, evidence and remediation.

## 6. Reporting
Compile validated observations into the final assessment report.

## 7. Stop Conditions
Stop immediately if scope becomes unclear, an unauthorized asset is reached, the approved window ends,
or testing could cause unintended impact.
